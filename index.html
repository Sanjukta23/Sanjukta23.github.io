About

I'm an embedded systems engineer finishing an MSc at the University of Leeds (predicted Distinction), most at home at the register level — writing bare-metal firmware, bringing up hardware, and chasing bugs down to the bit with a logic analyser and SWD.

My work spans a from-scratch STM32 driver library (no HAL), a custom UART bootloader with in-field update and failsafe recovery, and a distributed LoRa fault-detection network that became a filed patent application. I also work across Verilog/FPGA on a Cyclone V and Cortex-A9 NEON DSP.

I'm methodical and validation-first: I like to prove a design against requirements and against live signals before I trust it. Currently seeking graduate embedded / firmware / FPGA roles in the UK.
